Added support for communities.
`ZenodoClient.set_draft_community` selects the community for a draft
(the same as choosing one in Zenodo's upload form),
`ZenodoClient.get_draft_community` and `ZenodoClient.remove_draft_community`
read and remove that selection,
and `ZenodoClient.submit_draft_for_review` submits the draft to the community
(which publishes it if the community accepts it).
`ZenodoClient.get_community_id` turns a community's slug into its ID
and `Record.community_ids` gives the communities a record is in.
Adding a published record to a community (an inclusion request)
is not supported yet.
